Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Data you choose to provide

The app can store the profile name, handle, card design, and supported account links that you enter. Profile media and QR screenshots are accessed only after you select them. A QR screenshot is processed to extract the selected service link.

Nearby exchange and device security

Bluetooth and supported spatial sensors are used while the app is open to discover a nearby LiqSwip device and align a card transfer. The service uses an installation-scoped, pseudonymous record to prevent duplicate rewards and keep card ownership consistent. The raw installation secret is kept in secure device storage; the backend stores a one-way hash rather than that secret.

Network and account-link features

When you explicitly request a Wi-Fi sharing feature, the app uses Apple system APIs and a short-lived encrypted peer exchange. Account-link buttons open the corresponding app or supported destination; the destination service applies its own privacy policy.

How information is used

  • Provide card creation, nearby transfer, connection history, and seasonal card rewards.
  • Keep accounts and owned cards separated while recognizing the same installation.
  • Prevent replay, duplicate reward claims, and unauthorized ownership changes.

Sharing, sale, and advertising

Profile information is shared with another nearby user only when you perform a card exchange or another explicitly selected sharing action. The app does not sell personal information and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Retention and control

Local profile data remains on the device until you edit it, remove it, or delete the app. Server-side installation, exchange, and card-ownership records are retained as needed to provide security and reward integrity. You can request support or deletion guidance from the support page.
