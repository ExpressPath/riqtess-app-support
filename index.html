riqtess

Support

riqtess in LiqSwip Preview lets you design a digital profile card, attach supported account links, discover a nearby device while the app is open, and exchange cards.

Before requesting help

  • Update iOS and LiqSwip Preview to the latest available version.
  • Keep both phones unlocked with the app open during nearby card exchange.
  • Allow Bluetooth, Nearby Interaction, Photos, Motion, or Location only when the feature you chose needs it.
  • For app-link issues, confirm the destination app is installed and signed in.
